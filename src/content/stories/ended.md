Even when i cried  
All i can recall was  
Him being absent  
When he was with me  
All i can recall is me calling him…  
Yet him being absent

He tried, surely he must have  
Not like he didnt love me  
I mean he claimed that he DID  
And he promised to change  
And made versions of himself

He explained how the newer version is better one  
How the new him is based on the last fight  
And how he did make efforts of changing himself  
All on the basis of my prompts, of what i said

And surely he must have  
The changes were all so visible  
So much distinguishable  
That he made sure i counted them everyday

But then, again, the moment  
In every little inconvenience  
All his versions, with no delay  
Would morph them all together  
Left with where it all started….  
Leading (its way) to where it all ended…
