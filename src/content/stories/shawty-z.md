Stop me when you're done  
Hold on if its still fun

Kissing u in my dreams  
Each blink i see your lips

Bet, Lashes are the curtain  
So i hide my desires within

Let my words be the pursuit  
Are you still worth the wait?

Met you in your absence  
My dreams are still just your ambiance.

Are you actually warm  
Or i steeped too much of u in my thoughts

Dig it in, hear me scream  
Take it out, I'll lead you to my mouth

Nothing's permanent, so choose this best temporary-sss
