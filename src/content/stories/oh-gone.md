How happy i was, even with the vulnerability of losing you  
But this certainty is scratching the core of my heart that I've already lost u  
I was been told it'll hurt less as time passed  
But it hurts more, realising that the distance WOULD last

This distance, playing the streaks game with me  
Sharing 1 snap at a time, as reminder to our existence  
And the moments all in past tense  
Should i break it or continue?  
Should i let the glimpse go or pursue

The story of realizations that hurts & Reality that burns  
With each passing day, your absence has become the essence of love  
for me, the truth and it's bitterness has spread all through and through  
like tea in boiling water, here evaporates the hope and all the wills flee

Giving up the zeal, No complaints no agony, numbness is all i can feel  
Probably here starts the journey where even my wounds would heal  
Probably "moving on" was not the holy grail…

i still need to experience more of it  
more of raw emotions ache  
more of pain MORE OF YOU...  
calls never responded  
feelings never expressed  
Talks yet to fumble,  
Eye contacts yet to break  
Whispers yet to be murmured  
yearning yet to be composed  
Outcry yet to be unheard  
Tears yet to be rubbed unnoticed
