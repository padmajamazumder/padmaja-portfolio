I want to witnesses you being dead from inside  
Nobody to hold onto but just success…  
The chase people did for you  
And how you ran to escape  
Should be the last thing you regret

I really will the earth and destiny  
To make you realize  
How important and inaccessible human connections are  
And how helpless it feels to not have anyone  
to celebrate or to share the happy & sad moments with

I really will to see you cry for someone's presence  
like you made people do for you…  
I really hope to not see you bloom and be colourful  
But dry and dark  
Too dead from inside to even see the spark

I dont want to see you die  
But see you dead (from inside)  
Dont want to see you breathless  
But hopeless  
Dont want to see you fly  
But baseless  
Dont want to see you cry  
But voiceless  
Dont want you see you with closed eyes  
But vision less  
Dont want to see you raw  
But shield less

I need no connections with you anymore  
But the connections we had  
Is the shit you can't clean for the rest of your life  
It's not about getting hurt  
It's about breaking down  
Scattered and shattered into pieces

I surely would hold me up  
Once you drown  
I would breathe in relief  
Once you suffocate  
I would heal  
Once you bleed  
I would rearrange my pieces  
Once you burst

(I have spent 4 new years hoping the best for you  
Since 2020 till 2024 i wished the best for you  
I loved my extreme and gave you all my heart and soul  
And now the vice-versa begins  
Just how loyal and constant i was into loving you  
Is how great i would be at hating you  
Idk if i have unloved you  
Probably yes months back but now its the different zone  
Congratulations to ur efforts into landing me here  
Lets lead this way as per my convenience for the first time may be)
